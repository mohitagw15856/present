# Sky Check

Tonight's golden hour, sunset, civil dusk and tomorrow's sunrise for where you are, worked out on the device with the NOAA solar equations. No weather service, no lookups, no notifications. You have to remember to open it, which is the point.

- Position comes from the Geolocation API once (with your permission) or typed in by hand, rounded to a tenth of a degree (about 10 km) and kept in `localStorage`.
- No postcode lookup, because that would mean sending your postcode to someone.
