# Presence Kit for Venues

A six-step guide for cafés, pubs and community rooms on running a weekly phone-free hour, plus a poster generator (A3 or A4) with the venue name and time. Points at the other printables (parking table cards, noticing cards, placemats, quiz sheets) and the combined print pack.

The advice is deliberately low-key: pick a quiet hour, print, offer one small thing, do not enforce, tell people in the room rather than online.
